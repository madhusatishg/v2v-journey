**Key Observations from Baseline Code (25/03/26)**

🚫 Giving green signal alone is NOT sufficient
→ Vehicles must have a **clear exit path** to move effectively.

### 🔍 Observations

1. **Downstream Congestion Issue**

   * Even when the signal is green, if the **next lane (lane C) is congested**, vehicles cannot move.
   * This leads to **queue spillback**, blocking the intersection.

2. **Intersection Blocking Problem**

   * Vehicles enter the junction without ensuring exit availability.
   * This results in **gridlock**, especially near traffic lights.

3. **Third-Lane Interference**

   * Vehicles from other lanes aggressively try to merge into the same outgoing lane.
   * This **blocks the emergency vehicle**, even when it has priority.

4. **Emergency Vehicle Delay**

   * Despite signal priority, emergency vehicles get stuck:

     * At the **intersection**
     * Or at the **previous traffic light**

---

### 🎯 Key Insight

> 🚫 Green signal ≠ Guaranteed movement
> ✅ Green signal + Free downstream lane + Controlled merging = Effective movement

---

### 🛠️ Action Items (Next Steps)

1. **Check Next Lane Congestion**

   * Before giving green, verify if the **target lane has sufficient space**

2. **Improve Emergency Vehicle Prioritization**

   * Restrict competing lanes
   * Allow emergency lane exclusive or dominant access

3. **Intersection Clearance (Future Work)**

   * Ensure vehicles do not enter junction unless exit is free
   * Actively clear vehicles already inside the junction

---

Move from:

* Rule-based signal switching

To:

* **State-aware traffic control system**

  * Considers downstream conditions
  * Handles multi-lane conflicts
  * Prioritizes emergency flow intelligently
